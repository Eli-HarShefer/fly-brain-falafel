# Research imagery

`research_brain.png` and `research_em.png` are crops of Figure 1 of:

> Dorkenwald, S., Matsliah, A., Sterling, A.R. et al.
> **Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain.** *Nature* 634, 124–138 (2024).
> https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07558-y

The article is open access under **CC BY 4.0**, which permits reuse and
adaptation including commercially, provided the source is credited. The credit
is burnt into the video frame itself (`src/ui/photo.js`), not left to a
description that can be stripped when a clip is reposted.

`fig1.png` is the unmodified source figure, kept so the crops can be redone.

Nothing here comes from the HHMI film "The History of Fruit Fly Connectomics at
Janelia" or from the Google Research blog: both are all rights reserved.
